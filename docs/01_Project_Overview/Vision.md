# Project Vision — PlantHub Bangladesh

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft — Development Phase
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-06
**Prepared By:** Software Engineering Team

---

## 1. Vision Statement

> "PlantHub Bangladesh is not just an e-commerce platform — it is a complete digital ecosystem for nurseries, plant lovers, and plant experts, empowering the future of green commerce in Bangladesh."

PlantHub Bangladesh will modernize the country's nursery industry by replacing informal, fragmented sales channels (Facebook Pages, Messenger, WhatsApp) with a single, trusted, digital-first platform. It brings together three groups — nursery owners, plant buyers, and plant experts — into one connected ecosystem for commerce, consultation, and education.

## 2. Problem Statement

Today, most nurseries in Bangladesh operate through informal social-media channels:

- No structured inventory or stock tracking
- No reliable order or delivery management
- No centralized customer relationship history
- No standardized way for buyers to discover, compare, or trust a nursery
- No accessible channel for expert plant care advice
- No shared knowledge base for plant care in the local climate and context

This creates friction for both sellers (who cannot scale operations) and buyers (who cannot easily discover, trust, or get support for the plants they purchase).

## 3. Product Vision

PlantHub Bangladesh will be a **multi-vendor nursery commerce and management platform**, providing:

- A trusted **marketplace** where customers browse, compare, and purchase plants from verified nurseries
- A full **business management suite** for nursery owners — inventory, pricing, orders, customers, analytics, invoicing
- A **consultation system** connecting customers with verified plant experts
- A **knowledge hub** for plant care education and a creator economy around that content
- A foundation designed to support **future AI-powered features** (disease detection, recommendations, smart search)

## 4. Who This Is For

| User Role | Core Need PlantHub Solves |
|---|---|
| Customer | A trustworthy place to discover, buy, and get expert help with plants |
| Nursery Owner | A digital back office to run and grow their nursery business |
| Plant Expert | A channel to monetize expertise through consultation and content |
| Platform Administrator | Centralized control, verification, and oversight of the ecosystem |

## 5. Long-Term Direction

The platform is designed to grow in phases beyond the initial marketplace:

- **Phase 2:** Produce marketplace, plant passport/growth timeline, multi-branch nursery support, employee management, weather-based care reminders
- **Phase 3:** AI-powered disease detection, recommendation engine, smart search, business intelligence, and customer support

## 6. Why Now

- Rising urban interest in gardening and indoor plants in Bangladesh
- Nursery businesses currently underserved by e-commerce and SaaS tooling
- Growing smartphone and mobile payment (bKash/Nagad) penetration makes a mobile-first marketplace viable
- No dominant, trusted, nursery-specific platform currently exists in the local market
